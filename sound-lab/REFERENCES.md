# Sound Workshop reference brief

These links define direction only. No YouTube audio is copied, extracted, cached, or distributed by the game.

- Friendly radial engine: [Big Old RADIAL BIPLANE ENGINES Cold Start and Sound](https://youtu.be/bARUNJfMjuc), 1:40–2:00.
- Grenade mechanics and blast: [Grenade Sound Effect (NO Copyright)](https://youtu.be/UYqNUo7GioU).
- Sortie-complete call: [Retreat](https://youtu.be/6fgDxAASI_c), approximately the first three seconds.
- Main-menu atmosphere: [Tom Clancy's Ghost Recon — Gameplay (PS2)](https://youtu.be/FiCGmscP49Q), 3:00–3:20.

The workshop recreates the requested characteristics procedurally:

- Friendly and enemy engines use deterministic seven-cylinder, four-stroke firing pulses, RPM wander, propeller blade passage, exhaust noise, and mechanical texture. Enemy variants raise RPM and approach intensity.
- Grenades separately model pin resonance, spoon release, broadband blast, low-frequency pressure, dirt, and debris.
- The traditional Retreat composition is represented with freshly synthesized bugle harmonics; the referenced performance is not sampled.
- Menu variants are original 16-second minor-key loops using low pads, restrained percussion, and atmospheric noise. They borrow no melody or recording from the reference.
