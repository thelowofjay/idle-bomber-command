# Prototype game surface

Mode: Operate. Scope: the portrait mobile PWA game shell and its ready, playing, paused, and debrief states.

Audience: mobile idle-game players observing repeated sorties and making short upgrade decisions. Primary task: understand the operation at a glance, intervene when useful, and redeploy without losing context. Success means the first biplane loop is readable, tactile, and worth repeating.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A forward-airfield command fantasy whose aircraft, battlefield, and workspaces are recognizable before any number is read. Refuse the abstract idle dashboard and placeholder battlefield.

**OWN-WORLD:** Khaki dispatch paper, deep field green, signal red, condensed period lettering, realistic authored airfield and battlefield plates, and spare utilitarian controls.

**STORY:** Read the two currency balances, browse the next operation over a recognizable aircraft-lineup airfield, deploy across farmland into a trench network, receive a temporary debrief, then return to the command hub. Requisitions, Armory, Maintenance, and Flight Operations open as focused upgrade windows from the persistent bottom dock.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** On 390×844, a two-currency strip and centered brass title lead into the full-bleed bomber-lineup scene. A single operation selector and deploy action sit above a fixed four-button department dock; upgrade detail appears only after a department is opened. In battle, the trench and recognizable targets occupy the top while the grass runway anchors the bottom. Signature move: each aircraft grows from 18px ground scale toward a 40px cruise footprint, carries a compact 43×14 KITE-series tag only once clear of the runway, and follows nose-led runway, strike, recovery, and landing routes without a decorative path trace.

**FORM:** Sixth grounded direction, aircraft-recognition deck translated into an operable game shell. seed:1d307aa154da680d.
