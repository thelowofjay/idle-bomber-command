# Prototype game surface

Mode: Operate. Scope: the portrait mobile PWA game shell and its ready, playing, paused, and debrief states.

Audience: mobile idle-game players observing repeated sorties and making short upgrade decisions. Primary task: understand the operation at a glance, intervene when useful, and redeploy without losing context. Success means the first biplane loop is readable, tactile, and worth repeating.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A living aircraft-recognition card: the biplane remains a clean, unmistakable silhouette while the battlefield supplies real motion and consequence. Refuse the generic dark command-center dashboard and its decorative gauges.

**OWN-WORLD:** Dusty sky blue, sun-bleached paper, field olive, graphite ink, and signal vermilion. Compact workhorse sans, tabular operation numerals, thin plotted paths, clipped field labels, and recognition silhouettes; standard web buttons remain unmistakable controls.

**STORY:** Read bonds and force readiness, deploy, follow the aircraft's outbound and return legs, mark a fleeting priority target, then understand exactly what the operation earned and what one upgrade will change.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** On 390×844, a narrow status header sits above a dominant 390×560 battlefield canvas. The airfield anchors the bottom, trench line the top, and a plotted sortie trace connects them. A grounded command tray below holds one primary Deploy/Pause action and concise force status without covering the sky. Signature move: launching stamps a recognition silhouette and call sign onto the sortie trace, which advances with the actual aircraft.

**FORM:** Sixth grounded direction, aircraft-recognition deck translated into an operable game shell. seed:1d307aa154da680d.
